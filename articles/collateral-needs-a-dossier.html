Collateral Needs a Dossier

Transferability, redemption and liquidation are different claims and each can fail on its own.

Tokenised assets are moving from portfolios into lending markets. A price feed alone does not establish usable collateral.

Four separate questions

Can the asset move to the secured party? Can it be redeemed? Can it be sold during stress? Does the legal claim survive the failure of an issuer, custodian or chain? An evidence dossier should answer each question and disclose where the answer depends on another institution.
