Markets often describe tokenisation as a change in the form of ownership. The harder question concerns the continuity of the underlying right. A token can trade every second while redemption, voting, transfer and recovery remain dependent on institutions outside the ledger.

## The operational test

The useful test asks whether the holder can exercise the promised right when an intermediary fails, a module changes or a transaction remains unresolved. Liquidity cannot repair an incomplete claim.

The next generation of digital securities infrastructure will compete on rights continuity, evidence and recovery as much as issuance speed.
