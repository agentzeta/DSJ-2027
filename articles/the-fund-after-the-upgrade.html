The Fund After the Upgrade

A financial system must preserve obligations and completed effects while its software changes.

Upgradeable software creates a simple financial problem. The code can change while investor obligations remain alive.

Continuity as market infrastructure

Redemptions, elections, reservations and completed payments need identities that survive module replacement. Without that continuity, an upgrade can duplicate an effect, release a reserved asset or strand an investor in an obsolete process.
